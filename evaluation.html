Justice & The Next 250 · Website refresh

SITE EVALUATION & RECOMMENDATIONS

SITE — justice250.org (Wix) DATE — July 17, 2026 PAIRED WITH — interactive redesign prototype

The current site does its basic job — it explains the campaign and points people to the toolkit — but it reads as an early placeholder: one long text page, off-brand typography, navigation that doesn't navigate, and no home for the campaign's actual momentum (events, writing, film). The brand book is genuinely strong; the site just isn't using it yet. Below: what we found, and what to change, in priority order.

1WHAT'S WORKING

  • The core narrative is already good — "we know what's broken; the next 250 are about what we build" is clear and repeated well.
  • IBM Plex Mono appears in parts of the site, so one of the two brand faces is partially in place.
  • The participation offer is concrete: host an event, host a screening, apply for microgrants. That structure is worth keeping — it just needs stronger presentation.
  • Social channels and toolkit are linked and current.

2ISSUES FOUND

High

Navigation doesn't navigate

All three menu items — ENGAGE, ABOUT, WHY NOW? — link to justice250.org itself. Clicking any of them reloads the page instead of taking the visitor anywhere.

Link each menu item to a page section anchor (Wix: add an Anchor to each strip, then point menu items at anchors), and add UPDATES / EVENTS / MEDIA once the new sections exist. Keep a persistent GET INVOLVED button in the header.
High

No home for campaign momentum (the client ask)

There is nowhere on the site for events, articles, or video. A campaign site that never changes gives people no reason to return — and the campaign is producing all three.

Add three sections — Campaign Updates (Wix Blog), Events (Wix Events), and Media (YouTube embeds / Wix Video) — as designed in the prototype. Details in section 3.
High

Off-brand typography

The live site renders headings and body copy in Arial and Poppins alongside Plex Mono. Neither Arial nor Poppins is in the brand book — the system is Dingos Stamp (display) + IBM Plex Mono (everything else).

Upload Dingos Stamp to Wix (Site Design → Text theme → Upload fonts), set it as the heading face, set IBM Plex Mono for body/buttons/menus, and delete every Arial/Poppins override.
High

Key actions bounce visitors off-site to Google Forms

Microgrant applications and screening-package requests link out to Google Forms. That breaks the visual experience, loses the visitor, and none of the submissions land in the site's own contact database.

Rebuild both as native Wix Forms (branded, on-page or lightbox). Submissions then flow into Wix CRM with email automations — confirmation to the applicant, notification to Jenna.
Medium

No email capture anywhere

The single highest-value thing a campaign site can collect is an email list, and there is currently no way to leave one.

Add a newsletter signup block (Wix native email marketing, or Mailchimp embed) — designed into the Get Involved section in the prototype.
Medium

Wall-of-text layout; brand world unused

The page is long paragraphs on a plain ground. The brand's most distinctive assets — halftone texture, sky/clouds, stamp lockups, the ticker-tape edge motif, the three color ramps — are almost entirely absent, and the copy contains stray artifacts (orphaned commas as links, empty spacer lines).

Break copy into the poster-style sections shown in the prototype: pull-quote About, three "shift" cards for Why Now, card grids elsewhere. Every long paragraph should either become a card, a quote, or get cut by half.
Medium

Heading structure & accessibility

The page has no H1 and only one H2 ("HOW WILL YOU SHAPE THE NEXT 250?"). Link text like "HERE" and punctuation-only links hurt screen-reader users and SEO alike.

One H1 (the campaign name) in the hero; one H2 per section; descriptive link text ("Request a screening package", not "HERE"); alt text on every image.
Low

"SHOP NOW" appears without context

A SHOP NOW call-to-action shows up with no product, imagery, or explanation around it.

Either give merch its own small, branded section (with product photos) or remove the button until the store is ready to present.

3THE NEW SECTIONS — HOW TO BUILD THEM IN WIX

Campaign Updates (blog + news)

  • Install Wix Blog. Create three categories: News, Perspectives, Event Recaps — these become the filter chips shown in the prototype.
  • Show the latest 3–6 posts on the homepage as a card grid; full archive lives at /updates.
  • Seed it with five posts you can write today: the Lucasfilm premiere recap, microgrants announcement, PBS series announcement, a "why this campaign" essay adapted from existing site copy, and a toolkit launch post.
  • Each event should produce a recap post within a week — that's the content flywheel: event → recap → social → next event.

Events

  • Install Wix Events. It handles RSVP, capacity, calendar export, reminder emails, and automatically moves past events out of the upcoming list.
  • Feature the PBS screening program as the hero of this section (as in the prototype) — it's the campaign's strongest single offer through November.
  • List partner-hosted events too: a simple Wix Form ("Submit your Next 250 event") feeding a moderated list makes the campaign feel national.
  • After an event ends, link its card to the recap post in Updates so nothing dead-ends.

Media

  • Use YouTube embeds (or the Wix Video Gallery pulling from the @TheJustTrust channel) — don't upload video files to Wix directly; playback and analytics are better on YouTube.
  • Layout: one featured film large, supporting videos beside it, "See all on YouTube" beneath — as prototyped.
  • Give every video a mono-caption title and date so the section reads as a growing archive, not a one-off.

5HOUSEKEEPING

  • SEO & sharing: set page titles/descriptions, and build a branded og:image (stacked lockup on sky halftone) so links shared on social look like the campaign.
  • Performance: compress hero imagery, cap animation on mobile — the current Wix page is heavy enough that it renders slowly.
  • Analytics: turn on Wix Analytics + GA4, and give the toolkit UTM-tagged links so partner-driven traffic is measurable.
  • Dates: July 4, 2026 has passed — any "countdown to the 250th" framing should now point forward (PBS broadcast in November, then 2027 programming).
SUGGESTED BUILD ORDER

Week 1 — typography + navigation fixes, hero rebuild, section restructure. Week 2 — Wix Blog with five seed posts, Wix Events with the screening feature, Media grid. Week 3 — native forms, newsletter, countdown, ticker, analytics. Everything visual is already specified in the interactive prototype.
